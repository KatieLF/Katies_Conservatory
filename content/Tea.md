Inspired by [Gwern](https://gwern.net/review/tea), this list serves a way for me to keep track of what I like and don't like. My parents introduced me to Yorkshire English Breakfast at a young age, a tradition they brought back from their time in England. I did NOT like it at first but was eventually convinced with a chocolate almond coconut creamer. My palate has refined some and I have stronger opinions now.

In terms of sourcing, [Teasource](https://www.teasource.com/) is the brand I am familiar most with. (It also helps that it's Minnesota based and close to my current place of residence.)

## Black Tea

#### Top 5

- Witch's Brew
  "A lively mug of fig and pear fruits topped with aromatics of fermented hops." While I do not drink and was initially uncertain about the hops part, this has easily become my favorite brew for pricing and flavor.

#### Good Pile

- Yunnan Purple Beauty
  "Light in body, juicy in mouthfeel, no astringency."
  The Yunnan line at Teasource is quite good. This one has the body that I desire without lots of tannins getting in the way.

#### "Ehh" Pile

- Ceylon Sacred Peak
  "The liquor floats on a bubbly grapefruit-like sweetness, but lands on the freshness that only young garden greens possess." I did not get sweetness from this brew and the green-ness was not to my liking.

- Summer Cabin
  "Bright and light in mouthfeel with a honey and wildflower character." Definitely one of the lightest black teas I have tried. The wildflower character was not found by my mouth.

- Bear Claw
  "The cup has a gentle body and mouthfeel with the character of raisins, almond, and cinnamon." Like Summer Cabin, it was more gentle then what I wanted.

- Wondering Monk
  "A stoic sweetness lacks syrup and offers no astringency [with a] long exhaling floral finish..." For a while I tried out some black teas and that no astringency what so ever because I was tired of tea that sucked all the moisture from my mouth. While pleasant, it did not have the body I desired from a black tea

- The Winding Roads of Ceylon
  "Medium-bodied with a visceral texture, the cup delivers flavors of apples and fresh shrubbery with just enough astringency to keep everything bright and spritely. Grains and unripe bananas in the finish." Too green-like for my taste. Another sour tea when prepared in the English manner.
