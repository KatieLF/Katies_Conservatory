## Face recognition

Immich wiki says it can support importing face data in the asset metadata (exif/XMP)[Source](https://deepwiki.com/immich-app/immich/4.2-people-and-face-recognition) But doesn't go into detail on how that would work. It does not currently export face data.

You technically share each other photos but facial recognition will happen twice [Source](https://www.reddit.com/r/immich/comments/1ibpy22/comment/m9ls0i0/)

The [roadmap](https://immich.app/roadmap) says better sharing settings are on the way but no eta as to when. The [July recap](https://immich.app/blog/2026-july-recap) does say they are actually working on it!

Apple Photos seem to store the data in a db [Source](https://discussions.apple.com/thread/252747752?sortBy=rank) but on an apple computer, there is a specific file path [Source](https://apple.stackexchange.com/questions/373613/where-can-i-find-raw-data-for-apple-photos-face-recognition)
