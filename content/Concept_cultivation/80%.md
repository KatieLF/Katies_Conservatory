title: 80% effort gives you 100% more life
draft: True
---

As someone who used to wrestle with perfectionism quite strongly (and who is still in recovery), the rule of thumb that has gotten me past the mental hurdle is "80% done is better than 100% never started". My mind gets stuck in perfect mode, meaning I can never start because the conditions for the thing to happen have to be perfect. Through trial and error, and countless reminding's of my mother, I've gotten more the the point of "done is better than perfect".

"A ship that never catches any wind is still making a choice"

This is NOT to say that 100% shouldn't be given on certain things, but if giving 100% is stopping me from even starting....

This is a rule of thumb I mainly use in my own creative practice.
