# Preface

This is just a list of my favorite sourdough recipes for posterity. I've added the link but I tend to use my own process so I'm writing my method down word for word. I'm only allowed to keep 5 in here at a time so this lists doesn't get crazy long.

### [Chef John's Sourdough Bread](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/260540/chef-johns-sourdough-bread/)

"The tried and true basic"
100 grams active sourdough starter
394 grams bread flour
250 grams water
8 grams kosher salt

Measure out the water in the bowl, then mix in your starter (this helps spread all of the starter throughout the bread). Once incorporated, add in your flour and salt and mix until a shaggy dough forms.

Let it sit for 30 minutes, then do some stretch and folds. Repeat 3-4 times until the dough no longer stretches but breaks when you try to stretch it.

Let sit for 3-6 hours for a bulk ferment.

Gently place dough on counter and shape it to get some tension on top. Then place (NOT plop!) it upside down in a banneton or a bowel with a dishcloth. Cover and place in the fridge overnight (again, not necessary but a helpful step for flavor).

Once the dough is out of the cold environment, let it sit until room temp (for me, that's waiting an hour then starting the oven preheating). Set the oven to 450 Fahrenheit.

Gently flip the dough onto a baking sheet with parchment paper and score it.

Mist surface of the dough with a sprayer and pop in the oven with a source of steam (my favorite is another pan on bottom with boiling water). Bake for 25-30 minutes or until golden brown.

Let cool completely before slicing.

### [Sourdough Monkey Bread](https://makeitdough.com/sourdough-monkey-bread/)

"A crowd pleaser"
**Dough**
300 grams all-purpose flour
30 grams cornstarch
4 grams salt
113 grams sourdough starter mature and active
1 egg large
120 grams whole milk
60 grams granulated sugar
113 grams unsalted butter softened
9x13 pan
**Sugar Coating**
113 grams unsalted butter melted
9 grams vanilla extract
7 grams cinnamon ground
72 grams granulated sugar
67 grams light brown sugar
bundt pan

Combine everything but the butter in a large mixing bowl. Stir the mixture together until it forms a shaggy dough. Knead in the butter in 1 TB at a time.

Knead dough for 5-10 minutes until it is smooth. Put into a greased bowl and cover with plastic wrap and let rise until doubled.

Line a 9x13 pan with plastic wrap and flatten the dough on top until it fits the pan. Wrap with plastic wrap and cold ferment for 8-12 hours.

**Assembly**

Make the sugar topping by combining all ingredients.

Take dough out of the pan and put 1/3 of the sugar mixture into the bottom. Coat dough with melted butter and place butter-side down in pan. Coat top of dough with melted butter and sprinkle with 1/3 of sugar mixture. Divide dough into 40 potions (10x4 typically).

Brush bundt pan excessively with butter. Mix rest of butter with sugar mixture. Roll each section of dough into a ball and place in bundt pan, creating layers of dough and sugar butter mixture.

Cover with plastic wrap and let rise until doubled in size.

Preheat oven to 375 Fahrenheit. Once hot, place bundt pan on foil lined baking sheet (to catch sugar spills). Bake for 30-45 minutes or until the top is brown and the internal temp is 200 Fahrenheit.

Don't cool completely in bundt pan! It will stick.
